# Card asset licenses

## Fonts

[Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces), [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter), [JetBrains Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono), [Noto Sans Bengali](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Bengali), and [Noto Serif Bengali](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Serif+Bengali) are used by the app and are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). Bengali-friendly system fallbacks include Noto Sans Bengali, Nirmala UI, and Vrinda. No font files are bundled by the card components.

No third-party card photography or illustration assets are bundled. Recipient-provided photos are displayed as content and are not redistributed by the card components.

## Music

The default "Happy Birthday to You" recording by Pracchia-78 is hosted on Wikimedia Commons and marked Public Domain (Public Domain Mark 1.0) by its source listing. The original "Soft Wish" instrumental is synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API and uses no external recording or sample.
